Stepwise Standard Operating Procedure

Turn Around Timeline

IA identification for onboarding will be done by PMA / Cluster Expert / SDE based on Eligibility Matrix.

 

 

PMA/SDE will submit the details of Industry Association (IA) for in-principle approval.

 

Day T

SIDBI SDE will do the due diligence of IA and recommend/reject the application and inform PMA & PDIV.

 

PMA will do the need assessment of IA based on Sustainability Matrix.

 

T+2 days

Sustainability Matrix will be submitted to cluster expert/PDIV for concurrence of matrix and preparation of action plan.

 

T+4 days

PMA will submit the IA details for appraisal.

 

T+5 days

SIDBI SDE will inspect all the aspects of the proposal forwarded by PMA and offer its observations and comments.

 

If needed, SDE will also negotiate with IA/PMA for better value-for-money terms.

 

T+7 days

Cluster Expert will inspect the proposal and offer its observations and comments and forward it to HO Checker for circulation.

 

T+8 days

HO checker will confirm the appraisal and generate its approved PDF file, which will be circulated to sanctioning authority through SMART.

 

T+10 days

HO will advise the date of Committee meeting to SDE.

Appendix

 

Scope of Engagement of IAs with SIDBI under DIA

 

SIDBI's engagement with IAs will encompass the following key areas:

A.Institutional strengthening: governance reforms, customized action plans, and capacity building for IA leadership and staff.

B.Dedicated human-resource support: placement of Business Support Executives (BSEs) to drive member engagement and MSME support activities, with phased financial support from SIDBI for BSE deployment.

C.Digital enablement: access to the DIA portal (dia-msme.in) for member management, surveys, webinars, e-learning modules, dashboards, analytics and a knowledge repository.

D.Business Development Services: onboarding sector experts in finance, exports, DPR preparation, sustainability, compliance, marketing and productivity. An e-compendium of BDS providers on the DIA portal will enable matchmaking with IAs and members.

E.Credit facilitation and revenue generation: identification of credit-ready MSMEs, referral-based credit facilitation with SIDBI, and the creation of sustainable revenue models for IAs through value-added services.

F.Capacity building and networking: regular webinars, workshops, exposure visits and annual conclaves on themes such as energy efficiency, decarbonisation, circularity, exports, BIS, lean manufacturing, incentives and capital raising etc. An interactive learning hub, digital helpdesk and AI-enabled chatbot will support IA secretariats and MSME members.

G.MSME ecosystem development: stronger member engagement and retention, enhanced policy representation, and improved competitiveness for member MSMEs.
