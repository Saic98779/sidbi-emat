Annexure VII — SOP for onboarding IAs

  1. Identify the IA based on the eligibility matrix and submit details for in-principle approval.
  2. SIDBI SDE conducts due diligence and recommends or rejects the application.
  3. PMA assesses needs using the sustainability matrix; the cluster expert / PDIV concurs and prepares the action plan.
  4. PMA submits the appraisal; SIDBI SDE and the cluster expert inspect the proposal and record comments.
  5. HO checker confirms the appraisal for circulation to the sanctioning authority through SMART.

This is a process summary from the reference, not evidence that any approval has occurred.
